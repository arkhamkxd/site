The platform rebuild

Live

A WordPress site, replaced a page at a time — without ever taking it down.

The job
An ed-tech platform for children with special needs, running on WordPress, with twenty pages competing for the same search terms.
The rebuild
A custom content system — Handlebars, a local JSON store — shipped alongside the live WordPress site, so nothing ever went dark. 81 commits, May to October.
What it moved
Twenty competing pages consolidated into four. Organic traffic up 152% since May, at the site’s highest recorded click-through rate. Shipped the Arabic layer that opened a second-language channel.
What I found
Built the company’s first combined product, behaviour and search review. It surfaced a 69% activation gap nobody had measured.

Also in flight — a conversational assistant on Node and Cosmos, and the move from a 14-day trial to freemium, which I proposed and now lead. Neither live yet.
